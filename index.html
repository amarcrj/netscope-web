Network Performance Benchmarking

NetScope goes beyond raw signal listening. Test your real-world connection capability directly alongside RF metrics. Log your latency, download speed, and upload performance metrics correlated with signal variables at any given coordinates.

  • Ping & Jitter: Measure response consistency to verify connection stability.
  • Dynamic Speed Tracking: Observe speeds scale with RSRP and carrier aggregation changes.
  • Auto-Correlated Logs: Speeds are written with cellular parameter metadata in CSV rows.

Visual Drive Trails & Spatial Analytics

Map signals as you drive or walk. See signal drop-offs, tower handover points, and coverage dead zones with GPS coordinate plotting.

NetScope logs telemetry points with precise latitude and longitude. The app's integrated map automatically groups data and colors trail points depending on signal quality (Green = Great, Yellow = Fair, Red = Poor), providing immediate spatial analysis.

Trace physical tower switchovers. When your device changes serving Physical Cell Identifier (PCI) or splits across multiple carriers, the map flags the exact switch coordinates to highlight cells coverage edges.

Analyze how topography affects signal. Track elevation variables along your path to understand signal shading in valleys, behind buildings, and on elevated highways.

Privacy Policy

Transparency and privacy are fundamental to NetScope. We do not gather, share, or sell your telemetry or coordinate details.

1. Local Data Processing Model

NetScope is engineered on an on-device architecture. All cellular signal parameters (RSRP, RSRQ, SINR, PCI, Carrier, Network Type), GPS logs (latitude, longitude, elevation), and speed benchmark measurements are processed, visualised, and written strictly to local storage files (CSV format) on your device. We do not maintain or connect to any cloud server by default, and we never collect, compile, or transmit your records.

3. User-Configured Sync Options

NetScope provides an optional synchronization pipeline to Supabase. This sync operates directly between your device and your private Supabase database using credentials you supply. NetScope does not act as an intermediary, does not store your Supabase keys, and has no visibility into your database logs.

5. Guaranteed Anonymity & Zero Tracking

Every cellular parameter and location telemetry record captured by NetScope is completely anonymous. The app does not collect, link, or transmit any unique personal identifiers (such as IMEI, UUID, email, or device IDs) with the logged network variables, ensuring complete isolation between your metrics and identity.

2. Permission Rationale

To deliver its core utilities, NetScope requires specific Android or iOS hardware permissions, strictly utilized as follows:

  • Location Access (Precise/Fine GPS): Required to associate signal parameters with geographic coordinates and draw trails on the map views. Location data is stored purely locally on your device in your session CSV files.
  • Phone State Access: Required to extract raw hardware indicators from the active SIM cards (carrier name, network technology type, signal strengths, physical cell identifier PCI). NetScope never reads your contact list, device identifier numbers, or phone call history.
  • Notification Access: Used to run a persistent foreground service, allowing the background logging utility to write session data continuously when the screen is locked or when switching between active tasks.

4. Data Ownership & Deletion

You have full ownership of your records. You can view, export, or permanently purge any logged CSV data directly within the file log history tab of the app UI. Uninstalling NetScope deletes all local data folders and logs completely.

For privacy inquiries or technical clarification regarding hardware metrics extraction, contact the developer.
